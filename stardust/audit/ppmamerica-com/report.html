Executive summary

The research is there; the route out of it is not.

Conversion focus is the weakest dimension at 35, carries the heaviest weight (20) and is the biggest lever: research dead-ends leave no next step on 7 of 8 audited pages, and no page asks the reader to subscribe or to contact a strategy team (F-001). The front shelf also misdescribes the firm’s own products: on the hub, the CLO Equity Outlook card carries the Emerging Markets Debt excerpt and two other outlooks share one excerpt word for word (F-002).

The remaining P1s are access and indexing: 78% of images carry no usable alt text (F-003), the mobile menu button has no accessible name (F-004), and the XML sitemap returns HTTP 500 (F-005). Performance is the strongest dimension at 74 and served-text parity is perfect (strict 100 on every audited page), so every fix here is editorial or template-level, with no re-platform. P1

5 findings. Fix first. Each one costs conversations, trust or indexing today.

F-001P1Conversion focus

Research dead-ends: no next step on 7 of 8 audited pages

Observed
The only button on the hub is 'OUR STORY' in the bottom mint band, which leaves the research hub. The 3 stream listings, the blog article and the press release carry no button at all (5 pages), and the blog article ends on disclaimer small print. The two market-insight pages offer only 'DOWNLOAD THE FULL PDF'. No subscribe, no 'contact the team' and no related-insights module appear on any of the 8 pages (measurements.ctaInventory).
Impact
Institutional readers who finish a CIO letter or an outlook have no path to the strategy team, a subscription or a conversation, so research attention does not become a mandate conversation.
Fix
Add an end-of-article module to every detail template: 2-3 related insights in the same asset class, a link to the matching Investment Solutions strategy page, 'Contact the <strategy> team' and 'Subscribe to the CIO Letter'. On the hub, put one subscribe/contact ask in the first viewport.
F-002P1Content and LLM visibility

Featured cards on the hub show the wrong or duplicate excerpts

Observed
news-and-insights.png, Featured row 1: the 'CLO EQUITY OUTLOOK' card carries the Emerging Markets Debt excerpt ('Emerging markets debt has remained resilient...'). The 'EMERGING MARKETS DEBT OUTLOOK' card (row 1, right) and the 'HIGH YIELD OUTLOOK' card (row 2, left) share the same excerpt word for word ('Rising yields and risk-off sentiment created opportunities to selectively add high yield exposure...').
Impact
A regulated asset manager's front shelf misdescribes two of its own products. Due-diligence readers see a controls lapse, and answer engines extract the wrong summary for the CLO outlook.
Fix
Bind each card's excerpt to its article (the article's meta description or lede) instead of a free-text card field. Add an authoring check that rejects identical excerpts in one card set.
F-003P1Accessibility

78% of images carry no usable alt text; 67 have no alt attribute at all

Observed
117/150 images across the 8 pages have empty or missing alt: 67 with no alt attribute and 50 with alt="". This includes 92 of 109 visible content images: card thumbnails, banners, author headshots and the CTA photo (measurements.altCoverage, T-img-alt-empty).Source: check, check:img-alt
Impact
Screen-reader users get no description of the author headshots and article imagery. Images with no alt attribute are announced by file name, e.g. '2026-10-27_QI_CLO_620x275.jpg'.
Fix
Give author headshots alt text naming the person (e.g. 'Ogechi Nwachukwu, Chief Investment Officer'). Give informative thumbnails a short description. Set alt="" explicitly on decorative ones, such as the press-release chevron tiles and the stock banners. Never omit the attribute.

F-004P1Accessibility

The mobile menu button has no accessible name

Observed
At 375x667 the hamburger button (26x28px) has no text, no aria-label and no aria-expanded state (live hub, gate accepted).
Impact
Screen-reader users on phones hear only 'button' with no label. The whole site navigation sits behind that one control, so they cannot tell it opens the menu.
Fix
Add aria-label="Menu" plus aria-expanded/aria-controls tied to the off-canvas nav, and enlarge the hit area to at least 44x44px.

F-005P1Technical SEO

The XML sitemap returns HTTP 500 and robots.txt declares none

Observed
/sitemap.xml returned HTTP 500 during extract and again during this audit. /sitemap_index.xml returned 404. robots.txt is 'User-agent: * / Disallow:' with no Sitemap directive (measurements.sitemapValid).Source: check, check:sitemap
Impact
Search engines can find new CIO letters, outlooks and press releases only by following links. The Market Insights listing alone holds 39 cards, so new research is indexed late or missed.
Fix
Fix the AEM sitemap servlet so /sitemap.xml returns valid XML covering /news-and-insights/**, add 'Sitemap: https://www.ppmamerica.com/sitemap.xml' to robots.txt, and submit it in Search Console.

Uplift directions

Three directions for the redesign, each tied to the findings it resolves. Benchmarks were skipped in this run; see the degradation record.

A

Direction A: Tomorrow's version of the site you have today.

Same IA and the same teal/mint/Century-serif surface, with the P1/P2 defects fixed. Every detail page gets an end-of-article next-step module (related insights, strategy team, subscribe). Hub excerpts are bound to their articles. Alt text, <main>, a skip link and a named mobile menu are added. Section/card headings are re-levelled, a modular type scale and a 65-75ch measure are adopted, and listings get filters with a dated text list for press releases.

Resolves F-001 F-002 F-003 F-004 F-006 F-007 F-015 F-018 F-021 F-023

B

Direction B: What if we amplified the Key Takeaway?

The mint Key Takeaway band with its one-line serif thesis appears only on the blog article (news-and-insights-blog-2026-10-cre-market-valuations.png). Here it becomes the compositional spine. The hub leads with the latest CIO letter's thesis and portrait instead of the stock meeting photo. Every card leads with its one-sentence house view and named author. Mint becomes the deliberate 'house view' signal and teal the single action color. CIO letters and outlooks publish their key points in HTML above the PDF.

Resolves F-014 F-016 F-009 F-002

C

Direction C: What if motion was part of the identity?

Same IA as A. The editorial register suits a measured, professional-formal publication brand (PRODUCT.md observed personality, 'no playful motion' anti-reference). Motion is limited to slow reading-paced reveals of the takeaway line, chart lines drawing in on scroll for the data exhibits, and calm cross-fades between listing filters. Kinetic entrances are refused, and every move honours prefers-reduced-motion.
